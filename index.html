How to use this guide

A matched ballot, with a regional option

The default view was checked against the two-page November 3, 2026 official DuPage County sample ballot for 2524 Stonewall Avenue, Woodridge, IL 60517 (Precinct Lisle 034, Ballot Style 0589). It includes all 23 decisions and printed candidates from that PDF. The regional option also covers other Woodridge and nearby DuPage districts (including County Board District 3, Illinois House District 82, 4th Judicial Subcircuit, Downers Grove Township 708 mental-health levy, Lisle Park District bonds, and Lemont-Bromberek SD 113A limiting rate). The certified ballot controls names, eligibility, order, and vote limits.

Research snapshot: October 28, 2026. This guide covers printed candidates and the write-in spaces shown in the official sample ballot. The declared-write-in list could not be independently verified, and late certifications can change. Check the Clerk’s current candidate, retention, proposition, and write-in lists ↗.

Fair comparison means clear evidence

Campaign statements describe a candidate’s stated agenda; they do not prove an outcome. Records describe documented actions. “Not verified” means this research did not establish a position—it does not mean opposition, support, or indifference.

“Supporters’ case” and “tradeoff to weigh” identify arguments and implementation questions, not recommendations. Bipartisan examples are specific actions when verified; they are not a comprehensive collaboration rating. Judges are evaluated on qualifications and professional assessments, not predicted political rulings.

Your choices belong on your device

There is no app database, analytics, account profile, or network request carrying selections. The app saves choices and filters in this browser’s local storage. Another person using this same browser profile can see them. Clearing browser data can erase them; private browsing may not retain them.

Anyone with access to the guide who uses a separate browser or device gets their own checklist. Choices do not sync. The site host can still log ordinary page visits; it does not receive the checklist through app code.

Voting, accessibility, and other elections

For 2524 Stonewall Avenue, Woodridge, the assigned Election Day polling place is Belmont Bible Church (5430 Belmont Rd, Downers Grove, IL 60515), open 6 a.m.–7 p.m. DuPage County also provides early voting, Vote Anywhere, and mail voting information, including accessible voting equipment and assistance. Village of Woodridge mayor/trustees, Woodridge Public Library trustees, Woodridge Park District commissioners, Woodridge SD 68 / CHSD 99 / COD 502 board members, and Lisle Township officers are elected in the odd-year consolidated election (April 2027) rather than this November 2026 general election.
